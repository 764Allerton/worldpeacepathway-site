What the app is

World Peace Pathway is a quiet toolkit: meditation on the Journey path, Act for what you can do today, Connect for circles and the Sunday World Peace Pause, Reflect for private notes, and Grow for a steady ladder — not a score.

The iPhone app is in TestFlight under the earlier name while this site and the new Apple record are prepared. There is no public download yet.
