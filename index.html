Our mission

World Peace Pathway exists to help bring about world peace, one person at a time, through Higher Self practice and simple, caring technology.

The app

World Peace Pathway is a quiet toolkit on your phone, built in five parts:

Pathway

Daily Higher Self meditation, twelve series taken in order, one session at a time.

Act

A peace deed you can do today, for someone near you.

Connect

Circles of fellow practitioners and the Sunday World Peace Pause, a shared moment of silence.

Reflect

Private notes on what you notice in your practice.

Grow

A steady ladder of growth, not a score.
